What Do We Say To Guests? (Scripting Playbook)

Team Members should memorize these natural prompts. Listen to the order, identify the trigger, and offer one specific item before totaling.

Guest Response Transitions (Closing The Suggestion With Hospitality)

When Guest Says "Yes!": "Awesome choice, you'll love it! Your total is $12.45. It's our absolute pleasure to serve you today!"
When Guest Says "No thanks": "No problem at all! Your total is $9.20. Pull right forward to the window, and have a wonderful day!"

What We Need From The Team (The Two Golden Rules)

1

Specificity Wins — Retire "Anything Else?"

Asking open-ended, passive questions like "Will that be all?" or "Anything else for you?" triggers an automatic, subconscious "No." Always name a specific, craveable item (e.g., "large waffle fry", "Cookies & Cream shake"). Craveable words create visual appetite appeal!

2

The "One-and-Done" Rule & Speed of Service

We never badger guests or stack multiple upsells. Ask one natural, well-timed suggestion. If the guest declines, accept it cheerfully and instantly finalize the transaction. Never let a recommendation compromise queue flow or drive-thru speed.
